Nyx privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Nyx collects no data. Nothing you do in Nyx or in Safari is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Settings stay on your device

Your settings (the status, the schedule, your themes and anything you set for a particular website) are stored on your device.

How Nyx reads web pages

To darken a page, Nyx reads its stylesheets and images inside Safari, on your device. Some of them can’t be read directly, usually because they come from a different server than the page. In that case Nyx downloads them again from the same address the page used, so it can read their colors. Those are the only network requests Nyx makes.

iCloud sync

If you’re signed in to iCloud, Nyx syncs your settings between your devices with Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, in your own iCloud account. The developer can’t see this data. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

No tracking

Nyx has no analytics, ads, crash reporting or third-party SDKs, and it doesn’t track you across apps or websites.

Children

Nyx doesn’t collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy, open an issue at github.com/mohsen1/nyx/issues.

Nyx is open source under the MIT license.
