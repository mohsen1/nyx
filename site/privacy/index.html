Nyx Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Nyx collects no data. Nothing you do in Nyx or in Safari is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Settings stay on your device

Your settings, including the status, the schedule, your themes and your per-website settings, are stored on your device.

How Nyx reads web pages

To build a dark style for a page, Nyx reads that page’s stylesheets and images inside Safari, on your device. To read their colors, Nyx may fetch again only the stylesheets and images the page itself loads, from the same addresses the page uses. Nyx makes no other network requests.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Nyx syncs your settings between your devices using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot access this data. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

No tracking

Nyx contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party SDKs. Nyx does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Nyx does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome at github.com/mohsen1/nyx/issues.

Nyx is open source under the MIT license.
