Nyx Support

Get Nyx running and find answers to common questions.

Turn on Nyx on Mac

  1. Open the Nyx app once.
  2. In Safari, choose Safari → Settings → Extensions.
  3. Select the checkbox next to Nyx.
  4. Allow Nyx on all websites so it can darken every page. You can change this later under Edit Websites.

Turn on Nyx on iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Nyx app once.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  3. Tap Nyx and turn it on.
  4. Under permissions, allow Nyx on All Websites.

Once Nyx is on, open its menu from the extensions button in Safari’s address bar to change settings for the website you are on.

Common questions

Nyx is on, but pages are still light.

Check that Nyx is allowed on the website (see the steps above). If the status is Auto, Nyx only darkens pages while your device is in Dark Mode or during your schedule. Choose On to darken pages all the time. Pages that were already open may need a reload.

A website looks wrong with Nyx.

Open the Nyx menu on that website. You can try another theme, turn Nyx off for just that site, or choose Pause Until Tomorrow. Please also use Report a problem with this website or open a GitHub issue with the site’s address, so it can be fixed.

Why doesn’t Nyx change a website that already has a dark mode?

Nyx leaves websites with their own dark mode alone, because the site’s design is usually best. If you prefer Nyx’s theme, turn on Ignore built-in dark mode for that website in the Nyx menu.

Can I make my own theme?

Yes. In Nyx’s settings, open Themes and create a theme with your own background color, text color, image brightness and grayscale. You can share any theme as a link.

Do my settings sync between devices?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud. Nyx syncs settings through your own iCloud account.

Does Nyx collect my browsing data?

No. Nyx collects no data at all. See the privacy policy.

Contact

For help, bug reports and feature requests, open an issue at github.com/mohsen1/nyx/issues. Include your device, your OS version and the address of the website if the problem is with a specific page.
